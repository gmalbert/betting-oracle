How the edge is made

01

Data ingest

Fixtures, results, standings, weather and live odds flow in from ESPN, CFBD, KenPom and book feeds — cached, retried and cleaned automatically.

02

Model layer

XGBoost, LightGBM, Elo, Poisson and weighted ensembles — tuned with Optuna, tracked in MLflow, explained with SHAP.

03

Find the edge

Model probabilities meet market prices. Value bets, Kelly staking and nightly best picks — refreshed as the odds move.
